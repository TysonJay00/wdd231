The Hero

History of the Character

The Beginning

Bruce Wayne was born on February 19th, to Thomas and Martha Wayne. The Waynes were generationally weathly, being one of the families that built Gotham City. Thomas and Martha dedicated their lives to use their wealth to help the citizens of Gotham. Even though the family name was worth billions, Thomas got his medical degree and served people with their health, while Martha became a full-time philanthropist, raising money for charities. Thomas and his friend Lucius Fox, who worked at Wayne Industries, built infrastructure including transportation such as a train system to unite the city. When Bruce was eight years old, he fell into a nearby well, that was the entrance to a batcave. Bats screetched and flew around him, causing his life-long fear of bats. That same year, his parents took him to a theatre to view "The Mask of Zorro." When they exited the theatre, they walked through a back alley toward their car, but as they did so, a common thief, by the name of Joe Chill, pulled a gun on the three of them, and asked for their valuables. Fearful for his wife and son's life, Thomas tried to fight back, but Joe Chill shot Thomas, then Martha, and left Bruce all alone. Traumatized by this event, Bruce was never the same. He would carry the guilt of his parent's death and pain from that night forever. There was a darkness that overtook him. Filled with dread, he couldn't connect with anyone in school. The only connection he had, was with his butler, Alfred Pennyworth, who became a father figure and friend. growing up, he wrestled with what he wanted to do in life. He had a company, money, priviledge, but none of that mattered to him. He realized a piece of himself, underneath the guilt, the pain, and the dispair, was the desire to continue his parent's work to serve and protect the citizens of Gotham. But the world was different. The city became unhinged, more violent, more dangerous. The city needed something more than just money thrown around, especially with the criminal underworld growing since the Wayne's deaths. The city needed a symbol for hope. The citizens needed a symbol for justice, and the criminals needed a symbol to be afraid of. What the city needed, was "The Batman."

The Training

When Bruce became an adult, he disappeared from the world, leaving behind his wealth and home. He wanted to understand the criminal mind. He travelled all over asia and middle east. He starved, stole food, and was sent to prison. He knew that if would become a vigilante, he would have to have a line that he could not cross, to be the symbol he needed to be. He would not kill. That would be the difference between him, and his enemies. He travelled across mountains and through freezing weather to find masters of the art of martial arts. He would study dozens of martial arts, devoting himself every day and night until he was a master of each one. In school he was the most exceptional student. He could quickly understand any subject thrown at him, recieving the highest grades. He studied criminology, criminal science, and forensic science. He needed to assist the police, at least Commisioner Gordon, perhaps the only clean cop on the force. After years of studying and mastering what he needed to be a successful vigilante, he went back to Gotham City. He played a spoiled rich kid, who people would never expect to be Batman. Over the years, He found opportunites to help other kids who experiened similar tragedies. He took them in, and treated them as sons. He would train each one to help fight crime, and show them a better, happier path to move on from the pain, something he could never do himself. They woud have the alias, "Robin." The first robin would be Dick Grayson, then Jason Todd after Dick would move away. After Jason, Tim Drake would become the third robin. After Tim, then Damien, Bruce's own son, would become the fourth robin.
